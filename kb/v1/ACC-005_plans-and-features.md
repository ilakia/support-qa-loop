# Plans and included features

Kerbloop is offered in three plans. All plans include the web planner, the driver app and customer tracking links.

## Starter
- Up to 5 web planner users.
- Up to 15 active drivers.
- One depot.
- Tracking links and SMS notifications.
- Standard reports and CSV export.

## Professional
- Up to 25 web planner users.
- Up to 100 active drivers.
- Up to 10 depots.
- Everything in Starter, plus customer-chosen time windows (see TW-004), safe place and neighbour delivery options, Excel export and scheduled exports.

## Enterprise
- Unlimited users, drivers and depots.
- Everything in Professional, plus single sign-on, a personal account manager and custom tracking page branding.

## Checking your plan
Admins can see the current plan and how many users and drivers are in use under **Settings > Plan**.

## Changing your plan
To move to a bigger or smaller plan, an Admin clicks **Request plan change** under **Settings > Plan**. A member of the Kerbloop team contacts you to confirm the change.

## Questions this article does not answer
Prices, invoices, payments, refunds, contract terms, cancelling a contract and legal or data-protection questions are handled by people, not by the support bot. Use **Contact support** in the web planner and choose the topic, and the right team will answer you.
