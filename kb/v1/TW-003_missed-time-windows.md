# What happens when a time window is missed

Traffic, long stops or a breakdown can make a driver late. Kerbloop warns you early so you can act before the window closes.

## Warnings
- When the estimated arrival for a stop moves to **within 10 minutes of the window end**, the stop turns orange in the web planner.
- When the estimated arrival is **after the window end**, the stop turns red and is listed under **Live > At risk**.

## What you can do
- **Move the stop earlier** in the route with **Move stop**, if other stops have more flexible windows.
- **Move the stop to another driver** with **Reassign**, if a nearby driver has time.
- **Contact the customer** to agree a new window, then change it (see TW-002).
- **Keep the stop as it is.** The driver delivers late, and the stop is recorded as "Delivered late".

## Customer information
If SMS notifications are on, the customer automatically gets a message when the driver is expected to be more than 15 minutes late. The tracking page always shows the latest estimated time.

## Reports
Missed windows appear in the **On-time delivery** report, with the number of minutes late for each stop. Use it to find routes or areas that are often late.
