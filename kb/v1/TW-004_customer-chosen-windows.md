# Letting customers choose a time window

With customer time slots, the customer picks a delivery window on the tracking page. This reduces failed deliveries because customers choose a time when they are at home.

## Turn it on
1. An Admin goes to **Settings > Tracking page**.
2. Turn on **Let customers choose a time window**.
3. Add the slots customers can choose from, for example 08:00–12:00, 12:00–16:00 and 16:00–20:00.
4. Set the **Booking cut-off**: how long before the start of a slot customers can still choose or change it. The default is 2 hours.

## What the customer sees
On the tracking page, the customer sees the available slots for the delivery day and picks one. Slots that are already full or past the cut-off are greyed out.

## Capacity
You can set a maximum number of stops per slot for each depot. When a slot is full, it is no longer offered.

## What the dispatcher sees
Windows chosen by the customer are shown with a person icon in the web planner. They are treated as **hard** windows when the route is planned.

## Limits
- Up to six slots per day.
- Customers can change their slot up to three times per delivery.
