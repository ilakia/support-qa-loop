# Deactivating users and drivers

When someone leaves your company, deactivate their access straight away. Deactivating is safer than deleting, because the person's past work stays visible in reports.

## Deactivate a web planner user
1. Go to **Settings > Users**.
2. Open the user and switch off **Active**.
3. Click **Save**.

The person is logged out within one minute and can no longer log in. Their name stays on stops and routes they created. A deactivated user does not count towards the user limit of your plan.

## Deactivate a driver
1. Go to **Drivers** and open the driver.
2. Switch off **Active**.

If the driver has a route today, Kerbloop asks you to assign the route to another driver first. The driver is logged out of the driver app at the next sync.

## Reactivate
Switch **Active** on again. The person keeps the same role, depots and history.

## Deleting personal data
Deactivated users and drivers are kept so that reports stay complete. If a former employee asks for their personal data to be deleted, an Admin must contact Kerbloop support. Such requests are handled by a person, not by the support bot.

## Who can do this
Admins can deactivate users. Dispatchers and Admins can deactivate drivers.
