# Exporting reports

Kerbloop has ready-made reports for deliveries, drivers and depots. You can view them in the web planner or export them to work with them in a spreadsheet.

## Available reports
- **Deliveries**: every stop with status, times, driver and proof of delivery.
- **On-time delivery**: stops delivered inside, before or after their time window (see TW-003).
- **Failed deliveries**: failed stops per reason, driver and area (see FD-005).
- **Returns**: parcels at the depot and parcels to return to the sender (see FD-003).
- **Load check**: parcels that were not scanned at loading (see DRV-003).
- **Driver performance**: stops per hour, distance driven and working time per driver.

## Export a report
1. Go to **Reports** and open a report.
2. Choose the date range and, if needed, depots and drivers.
3. Click **Export** and choose **CSV** or **Excel (XLSX)**.

Exports with up to 50,000 rows download straight away. Larger exports are prepared in the background, and you get an email with a download link that is valid for 7 days.

## Scheduled exports
Admins can send a report automatically by email every day, week or month under **Reports > Scheduled exports**.

## Who can export
Admins, dispatchers and viewers can export reports. Users limited to certain depots only get data for those depots.

## Date range
You can export data from the last 24 months. Older data is no longer available in reports.
