# Adding users to your company account

Users are the people who work in the Kerbloop web planner, for example dispatchers and depot staff. Drivers are not users. They are added separately under **Drivers** and log in to the driver app (see DRV-001).

## Invite a user
1. Go to **Settings > Users**.
2. Click **Invite user**.
3. Enter the person's work email address.
4. Choose a role (see ACC-002) and, if needed, the depots the person may see.
5. Click **Send invitation**.

The person gets an email with a link to set a password. The link is valid for 72 hours. If it expires, open the user in the list and click **Resend invitation**.

## Who can invite users
Only Admins can invite users and change their roles.

## Adding a driver
1. Go to **Drivers > New driver**.
2. Enter the driver's name and mobile number.
3. Choose the depot and default vehicle type.
4. Click **Save**. The driver gets an SMS with the company code and a temporary PIN.

Dispatchers and Admins can add drivers.

## How many users you can add
The number of web planner users depends on your plan (see ACC-005). Drivers are counted separately.

## Single sign-on
Companies on the Enterprise plan can let users log in with their company account instead of a Kerbloop password. Setting this up is done together with your account manager.
