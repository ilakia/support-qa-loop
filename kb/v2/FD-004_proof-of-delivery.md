# Proof of delivery

Proof of delivery (POD) shows that a parcel was really handed over or left at the agreed place. It helps when a customer says a parcel never arrived.

## Types of proof
- **Signature**: the recipient signs on the driver's phone screen.
- **Photo**: the driver takes a photo of the parcel at the door or safe place.
- **Name of recipient**: the driver types the name of the person who took the parcel, for example a neighbour.
- **GPS position and time**: saved automatically for every completed stop.

Admins choose which proof is required under **Settings > Delivery options > Proof of delivery**. You can require different proof for different stop types, for example a signature for high-value parcels.

## Finding the proof
In the web planner, open the stop and go to the **Proof** tab. You see the signature or photo, the recipient name, the time and a small map with the GPS position. Click **Download PDF** to get a proof-of-delivery document you can send to a customer or shop.

## How long proof is kept
Photos and signatures are stored for 90 days. After that, only the time, GPS position and recipient name are kept.

## Customer says the parcel did not arrive
1. Check the proof of delivery for the stop.
2. Compare the GPS position with the address on the map.
3. If the proof looks wrong, contact the driver through the depot manager.
