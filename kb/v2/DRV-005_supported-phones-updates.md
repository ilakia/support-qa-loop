# Supported phones and app updates

The Kerbloop driver app runs on Android phones and iPhones. Drivers download it free from the Google Play Store or the Apple App Store by searching for "Kerbloop Driver".

## Minimum requirements
- **Android**: version 11 or newer, at least 3 GB of RAM.
- **iPhone**: iOS 16 or newer.
- A working camera for scanning and photos.
- GPS turned on and location access set to **Always** or **While using the app**.
- At least 1 GB of free storage for routes, photos and maps.

Older phones may install the app but are not supported. Problems on them are not investigated.

## App updates
We release a new version of the driver app about every two weeks. Drivers should turn on automatic updates in the app store.

When a version is too old to work safely, the app shows **Update required** at login and cannot be used until it is updated. Admins can see the app version of every driver under **Drivers > App version**.

## Company phones
If your company uses managed company phones, your IT team can install and update the app through its device management system. Ask your IT team for help with that system, as Kerbloop support cannot access it.

## Battery
Navigation and GPS use a lot of battery. We recommend a car charger for routes longer than four hours.
