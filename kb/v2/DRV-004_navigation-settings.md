# Navigation settings in the driver app

The driver app shows the route on a map and gives turn-by-turn directions to each stop. Drivers can change how navigation works under **Menu > Navigation**.

## Built-in or external navigation
- **Kerbloop navigation** (default): directions are shown inside the driver app. The driver does not have to switch apps, and the dispatcher sees the exact position.
- **Google Maps** or **Apple Maps**: the app opens the external map app with the next stop. After arriving, the driver switches back to Kerbloop to complete the stop.

## Vehicle type
Choose **Car**, **Van**, **Cargo bike** or **Truck**. Vans and trucks avoid roads with height or weight limits where the map has this information. Cargo bikes may use bike lanes and pedestrian zones where allowed. The vehicle type is set by the dispatcher on the route but can be changed by the driver for the current day.

## Avoid options
Drivers can switch on **Avoid tolls**, **Avoid ferries** and **Avoid motorways**. These options affect directions only. They do not change the order of stops, which is set by the dispatcher.

## Voice and display
- Voice directions on or off, and voice language.
- **Night mode**: a dark map, on automatically after sunset or always on.
- **Keep screen on** while navigating.

## Following the planned order
The app always suggests the next stop in the planned order. Drivers can choose another stop, but this changes the arrival times for the following customers (see TW-005).
