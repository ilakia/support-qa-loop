# Roles and permissions

Every web planner user has exactly one role. The role decides what the person can see and change.

## The roles
| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | Everything, including company settings, users, service areas, delivery options and the tracking page |
| **Dispatcher** | Create and edit stops and routes, assign drivers, add and edit drivers, use the address book, see reports |
| **Depot staff** | Check in returns, mark parcels as collected, see the stops and routes of their depot |
| **Viewer** | See routes, stops and reports. Cannot change anything |

## Limiting users to depots
Dispatchers, depot staff and viewers can be limited to one or more depots. They then only see stops, routes and drivers of those depots. Admins always see all depots.

## Changing a role
1. Go to **Settings > Users**.
2. Open the user.
3. Choose the new role and click **Save**.

The change takes effect the next time the person loads a page. Only Admins can change roles.

## At least one Admin
Every company account must have at least one active Admin. You cannot remove the Admin role from the last Admin. We recommend having two Admins, so someone can still manage the account during holidays.

## Drivers
Drivers do not have a role. They only use the driver app and cannot log in to the web planner.
