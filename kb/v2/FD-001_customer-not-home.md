# When the customer is not home

If nobody opens the door, the driver records a failed attempt in the driver app. What happens next depends on your company's delivery settings.

## What the driver does
1. Ring or knock and wait at least 2 minutes.
2. Call the customer from the stop screen, if a phone number is saved.
3. If the settings allow it, try a **neighbour delivery** or a **safe place** (see below).
4. If none of this works, tap **Not delivered** and choose the reason **Customer not home**.

## Neighbour and safe place
- **Neighbour delivery**: the driver hands the parcel to a neighbour and enters the neighbour's name and house number. The customer is told where the parcel is by SMS and on the tracking page.
- **Safe place**: the driver leaves the parcel at a place the customer chose on the tracking page, for example "behind the garden gate", and takes a photo.

Admins turn these options on or off under **Settings > Delivery options**.

## What happens next
Kerbloop automatically plans the stop again for the next working day. A parcel gets **up to 3 delivery attempts in total**. After the last failed attempt, the parcel is marked **Return to depot** (see FD-003).

## What the customer sees
The customer gets an SMS with a link to the tracking page. There they can pick a new time window, choose a safe place or ask for a neighbour delivery for the next attempt.
